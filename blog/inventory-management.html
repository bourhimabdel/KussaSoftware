Inventory Management Guide

Inventory management is one of the most important parts of running a successful business.

What is inventory management?

It is the process of tracking stock levels, orders, sales, and deliveries.

Why it matters

  • Prevents stock shortages
  • Reduces waste and loss
  • Improves cash flow

Best practices

  • Use a POS system
  • Track sales in real time
  • Automate restocking alerts

Conclusion

Good inventory management helps businesses save money and operate more efficiently.
