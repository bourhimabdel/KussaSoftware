Request Data Deletion

Want to delete your KUSOX data? We're here to help. Send our support team an email explaining which data you want us to delete.

What would you like to delete?

Please specify exactly what you want removed from your KUSOX account. For example:

  • A project: a specific project and its associated data.
  • Project data: products, sales, tickets, or other information associated with a project.
  • Your account: your KUSOX account and associated data.

If your request concerns a specific project, please include its name or ID so we can identify it.

Send your deletion request to

support@kusox.com

Required email subject:
KUSOX Data Deletion Request

✉ Send Deletion Request
Important: Send your request from the same email address you use to sign in to the KUSOX application. This helps us verify ownership of your account.

Never include your password in your email. Your request will be reviewed by our support team. Sending an email does not automatically delete your data.
